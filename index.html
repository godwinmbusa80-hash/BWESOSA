Welcome to BWESOSA

Where Memories Become Legacy

BWESOSA brings together former students of Bwera Secondary School from different generations, professions and parts of the world.

Our association exists to strengthen relationships among former students, support one another, mentor young people and contribute to the continued development of our school and community.

Who We Are

About BWESOSA

Building a stronger alumni community through friendship, service, mentorship and shared responsibility.

Our Mission

To unite Bwera Secondary School alumni and mobilize their skills, resources and networks for the benefit of members, students, the school and society.

Our Vision

A united, successful and impactful Bwera Secondary School alumni community contributing to national development.

Our Values

Unity, integrity, service, excellence, friendship, responsibility and commitment to future generations.

Message from the President

"Our school gave us knowledge, friendships and a foundation upon which many of us have built our lives. BWESOSA gives us an opportunity to return that gift."

We invite every old boy and old girl of Bwera Secondary School to reconnect, participate and contribute to the future of our school community.

— President, BWESOSA
